This theme supports rendering beautiful math in inline and display modes using [MathJax 3](https://www.mathjax.org/) engine. You just need to surround your math expression with `$$`, like `$$ E = mc^2 $$`. If you leave it inside a paragraph, it will produce an inline expression, just like \\(E = mc^2\\).

To use display mode, again surround your expression with `$$` and place it as a separate paragraph. Here is an example:<br /><br />\\\[ \\sum\_\{k=1\}^\\infty \|\\langle x, e\_k \\rangle\|^2 \\leq \\\|x\\\|^2 \\\]

You can also use `\begin{equation}...\end{equation}` instead of `$$` for display mode math. MathJax will automatically number equations:

\\begin\{equation\} \\label\{eq:cauchy-schwarz\} \\left( \\sum\_\{k=1\}^n a\_k b\_k \\right)^2 \\leq \\left( \\sum\_\{k=1\}^n a\_k^2 \\right) \\left( \\sum\_\{k=1\}^n b\_k^2 \\right) \\end\{equation\}

and by adding `\label{...}` inside the equation environment, we can now refer to the equation using `\eqref`.

Note that MathJax 3 is [a major re-write of MathJax](https://docs.mathjax.org/en/latest/upgrading/whats-new-3.0.html) that brought a significant improvement to the loading and rendering speed.
