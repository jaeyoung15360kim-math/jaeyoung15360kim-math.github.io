  1. Irreducibly $SU (2)$-covariant quantum channels of low rank

    Euijung Chang, Jaeyoung Kim, Hyesun Kwak, Hun Hee Lee, Sang-Gyun Youn

    Reviews in Mathematical Physics, 34(07), 2250021 (2022)

    arXiv

    Abstract

    This paper studies quantum channels of rank at most three with SU(2) symmetry. It characterizes PPT and entanglement-breaking behavior, investigates degradability, derives Holevo and coherent information, and identifies a strong nonadditivity phenomenon for coherent information.
